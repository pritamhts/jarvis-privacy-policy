Privacy Policy for JARVIS

Effective date: November 1, 2026

JARVIS – Personal AI Assistant ("JARVIS", "we", "our", or "the app") provides AI assistant features, including online and offline AI processing, voice input, account login, and optional cloud synchronization. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, JARVIS may handle the following:

  • Account information: Email address, account identifier, display name, and authentication information provided through Firebase Authentication or Google Sign-In.
  • Device information: An app-generated device identifier, device brand or model, Android version, and related app/device information used for account and device synchronization.
  • Chats and settings: Chat history, app preferences, model settings, and other information you choose to synchronize.
  • AI provider credentials: API keys and related settings that you enter for supported AI providers. These may be stored in the app's local preferences.
  • Voice input: Audio or speech input when you activate microphone or voice features.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate your account and provide sign-in features.
  • Provide AI assistant responses and process your requests.
  • Enable optional cloud synchronization of chats and settings.
  • Maintain app functionality and manage connected devices.
  • Provide voice input and related assistant features.
  • Respond to support requests and improve app reliability.

3. Online and Offline AI Processing

When you use online AI features, your prompts and relevant conversation context may be sent to the AI provider you select, such as Google Gemini or a provider accessed through OpenRouter. Those providers process the information under their own privacy policies and terms.

When you use the offline AI feature powered by LiteRT-LM, prompts are processed locally on your device using the available local model. However, if you enable cloud synchronization, synchronized information may still be uploaded to cloud services.

4. Cloud Services and Third Parties

JARVIS may use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Sign-In, Google Gemini, OpenRouter and its selected model providers, and Android speech recognition services.

Information is shared with these services only as needed to provide the features you choose, such as authentication, cloud sync, online AI processing, or voice recognition. Each third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy and data practices.

5. Cloud Synchronization

Cloud synchronization is an optional feature. When enabled, selected information such as chat history, settings, and account/device-related records may be stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore and retrieved for synchronization across supported sessions or devices.

6. API Keys and Local Storage

API keys that you enter for supported AI providers may be stored locally in the app's preferences. Do not share your API keys with other people. You are responsible for managing and protecting the keys you provide.

7. Microphone and Voice Features

JARVIS may request microphone permission when you use voice input or related voice features. Speech recognition may be performed through Android or an available speech recognition service. The processing method may depend on your device and selected service.

8. Data Security and Retention

We use the security features provided by the app platform and connected services. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete information already stored in cloud services or by third-party providers.

9. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You can choose whether to use optional cloud synchronization and online AI services. You can also manage provider settings and API keys through the app where those controls are available.

If you use the account deletion feature, JARVIS attempts to delete associated cloud profile, device records, and synchronized chats or settings before deleting the Firebase Authentication account. Deletion by JARVIS does not automatically remove information retained independently by third-party providers or where retention is required by law.

10. Children's Privacy

JARVIS is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

11. International Data Processing

Our third-party service providers may process or store information on servers located in different countries, subject to their own privacy and security practices.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
